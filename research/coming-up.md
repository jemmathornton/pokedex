# Coming up: 10 Nov 2026 to end of Feb 2027

What’s announced (or rumoured) that could add something to the tracker, for a player in the UK: local raids only (no remote passes), global and ticketed global events, some in-person events in Europe. Checked 10 Nov 2026 against the app’s data (costumes.json, special-backgrounds.json, events.json, dex.json), read only.

**Confirmed** = on pokemongo.com. **Leek Duck** = on Leek Duck but not found in an official post. **Rumoured** = datamined or reported, not announced. **Not announced** = only the date is known, or nothing at all is known yet.

Events that add nothing to the tracker are left out: Spotlight Hours, Raid Hours, Cresselia, Darkrai, Deoxys, Shadow Tornadus, Mega Banette and Mega Metagross raids, GO Pass: November, the adidas research (Lucario), and Black Friday.

### Pokémon Night Out: London (in person, The O2), Tue 10 Nov 2026 (tonight)
Confirmed. Only for people at the venue. Pikachu (1★ raid) and Gengar (3★ raid) caught there have the London Location Background. Timed Research gives Toxtricity (Amped and Low Key).
- **Already in the app** (`night-out-2026-london`). Toxtricity is listed in the app as a background Pokémon, but the official post only gives the background to the raid Pokémon (already noted under `unsure`). Tick “I went” only if she went.

### Into the Wild (global), Mon 9 Nov to Fri 13 Nov 2026
Confirmed. Pokémon caught from 5★ raids (Crowned Sword Zacian and Crowned Shield Zamazenta, until 13 Nov) or 5★ Max Battles have a chance of the GO Wild Area 2026 Special Background. The free GO Pass gives Modern Jacket Machamp at Rank 75 (until 15 Nov). Dynamax Meltan comes from the GO Pass.
- **Already in the app**: background `sb-go-wild-area-2026-into-the-wild-go-wild-area-global`; costume `68-wildarea-2026-01` (Machamp, Modern Jacket); Dynamax Meltan and Regidrago.

### Gigantamax Machamp Timed Research (global, free), to Sun 15 Nov 2026
Confirmed. Walk 5 km for a Gigantamax Machamp, with a 1 in 20 chance of a shiny one.
- **Already in the app** (Machamp Gigantamax box).

### Pokémon GO Wild Area: Global (global, optional ticket), Sat 14 to Sun 15 Nov 2026, 10:00–18:00
Confirmed.
- Debuts: **Gigantamax Duraludon** (6★ Max Battles, both days), **Dynamax Dialga** (Sat), **Dynamax Palkia** (Sun). All can be shiny.
- Costume: Machamp in a modern jacket in 3★ raids, can be shiny.
- Special Background (GO Wild Area 2026) after a catch from a raid or Max Battle: Modern Jacket Machamp, Dynamax Dialga, Dynamax Palkia, Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem and Gigantamax Duraludon. The ticket’s Special Research also gives a Gigantamax Duraludon with the background.
- 5★ raids: Zekrom and White Kyurem (Sat), Reshiram and Black Kyurem (Sun). Ticket: Latias, Latios and Unown D, I, L, W from Incense.
- **Already in the app**: the background (all seven are listed), the costume, the Gigantamax Duraludon box, and the Dynamax Dialga and Palkia boxes.

### Minior Showers: Leonids (global), Sat 14 to Thu 19 Nov 2026, more Minior from 17:00 to 21:00 each day
Confirmed. Minior is found as Meteor Form. Its core shows only when its shell breaks in battle. One new core colour each shower: Green came with the Orionids (Oct); the Leonids colour is not announced. No shiny Minior has been announced.
- **App needs a fix**: dex.json counts all seven core colours as out, but only Green is out (plus whichever colour comes with the Leonids). It’s also unclear whether GO’s Pokédex counts each core colour separately. Better set each colour to not out in `release-fixes.json` until it debuts.

### Dynamax Trails (global), Mon 16 Nov to Mon 23 Nov 2026
Confirmed. Pokémon caught from Max Battles during the event have a chance of a **Special Background**. The GO Pass also gives event-themed Dynamax Pokémon with it (more on Deluxe). Not yet known which background it is or which Pokémon. Eternatus comes from the free GO Pass (once only). Eternamax Eternatus Max Battles are Sat 21 Nov, 11:00–14:00 (it can’t be caught).
- **To add when it’s out**: the background. If it reuses the GO Wild Area 2026 card, add the Pokémon to that entry; if it’s a new card, it needs its own `sb-` entry. Eternatus is already in the app.

### Max Mondays: Dynamax Espurr (Mon 23 Nov), Dynamax Cryogonal (Mon 30 Nov); 16 Nov to be announced
Leek Duck. Espurr isn’t on Serebii’s Dynamax list or in the app, so this looks like its **Dynamax debut** (not officially called a debut).
- **To add when it’s out**: Dynamax for Espurr (and Meowstic). Re-running the import should pick it up. Cryogonal is **already in the app**.

### Pawniard Community Day (global), Sat 21 Nov 2026, 14:00–17:00
Confirmed. Pawniard with a **Special Background** from the wild, Lures, some Field Research, Community Ambassador check-ins, and the US$1.99 Special Research (3 guaranteed). Kingambit learns Kowtow Cleave.
- **Already in the app**: Pawniard is on `sb-community-2026`. Shiny Pawniard is already out.

### Twilight Wishes (global), Tue 24 Nov to Mon 30 Nov 2026
Confirmed. Costumes, all can be shiny: Pikachu in a sun crown and in a moon crown (1★ raids), Espeon in a sun scarf and Umbreon in a moon scarf (3★ raids), Gengar Spooky Festival and Drifblim Halloween Mischief (Field Research from 26 Nov). No backgrounds, no debuts.
- **Already in the app** and on her costume list: `25-gofest-2024-stiara`, `25-gofest-2024-mtiara`, `196-gofest-2024-sscarf`, `197-gofest-2024-mscarf`, `94-fall-2022-noevolve`, `426-halloween-2021-noevolve`. A good second chance at any she’s missing.

### Clefable Super Mega Raid Day (global, optional ticket), Sat 28 Nov 2026, 14:00–17:00
Confirmed. **Mega Clefable debuts**, with a better chance of shiny Clefable. Super Mega Raids are only at some Gyms and need a group.
- **Already in the app** (Clefable has its Mega box from Leek Duck’s list).
