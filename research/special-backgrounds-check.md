# Special backgrounds: fact check (10 Nov 2026)

Checked against Leek Duck event pages, the official pokemongo.com posts, Pokémon GO Hub, Nintendo Wire and Serebii (Bulbapedia blocked the fetch). Full notes and sources per background: `data/special-backgrounds-check.json`. Nothing in `data/special-backgrounds.json` has been changed.

## Corrections

- **Team Leader Red**: add **Ponyta (Candela accessory)**. It had the Valor background from the Triumph Together research (Aug 2024) and the Candela’s Quest for Victory GO Pass (free rank 30, 9–15 Jun 2026). Neither was limited to Valor players.
- **Team Leader Blue**: add **Lapras (Blanche accessory)**. It had the Mystic background from Triumph Together and the Blanche’s Quest for Knowledge GO Pass (free rank 30, 26 May–1 Jun 2026).
- **Community Days 2026**: **Pawniard’s Community Day is 21 Nov 2026**, so it isn’t out yet.
- **GO Tour 2025 Black / White**: the species are right, but the note is wrong. Snivy, Tepig and Oshawott didn’t come from a “Pokémon Day” event. They came from the GOTOURUNOVA promo-code research (27 Feb–9 Mar 2025): you picked one starter, and its encounters came Black then White.
- **GO Fest 2025 Dark Skies** (suggestion): Power Spots at Max Finale could also have any Dynamax or G-Max Pokémon, and Serebii lists 23 more species (Pikachu, Growlithe, Abra, Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan, Rhyhorn, Electabuzz, Magmar, Eevee, Hoothoot, Hitmontop, Ralts, Trapinch, Combee, Roggenrola, Woobat, Deino, Inkay, Bounsweet, Drampa, Toxel, Sizzlipede, Duraludon).
- **GO Tour 2026 X / Y**: the lists are right. The note needs this: the Kalos starters came from the GOTOURKALOS code research. You chose one starter and got six encounters (X, Y, X, Y, X, Y), which is why she has Fennekin with both.

## Confirmed

- **Road to Kalos**: Gold, Silver, Ruby, Sapphire, Diamond and Pearl all match the official post. Each region had its own day (23–27 Feb 2026). Any raid catch that day had a chance of a background, but research encounters never got one. The legendary always got its own version (Shadow Ho-Oh Gold, Shadow Lugia Silver, Groudon Ruby, Kyogre Sapphire, Dialga Diamond, Palkia Pearl, both Origin Formes included). The costume Pikachu could get either version of the day, and no source says how that was picked. Serebii’s plain Ho-Oh and Lugia are wrong: only the Shadow ones got these backgrounds.
- **GO Tour 2025 Black White** (fused Kyurem) and **GO Tour 2026 Mega**. Dragonite was a Super Mega Raid, and Mega Finale Mewtwo has this Mega background.
- **GO Fest 2024**: the Wormhole background is right.

## Depends on a choice

- **GO Fest 2024 Radiance / Umbra**: you got Solgaleo or Lunala from the ticket’s research path. She picked Solgaleo, so Radiance and Wormhole Sun are possible for her; **Umbra and Wormhole Moon are not**.
- **GO Tour 2026 X / Y**: the guaranteed Xerneas or Yveltal followed your path, and the starter was one of your choice. Raid Pikachu, Honedge, Xerneas and Yveltal weren’t tied to your path as far as any source says.
- **Black / White starters**: one starter of your choice.
- **Team Leader Yellow**: at Ultra Unlock (Jul 2026) the colour followed your own team, so she got Yellow.
- **Delightful Days**: the G-Max Galar starter was one of your choice. **GO Fest 2025 Regis**: the research Regi was your choice.

Every background on the list could be had playing globally.

## Still unsure

- How the Road to Kalos Pikachu got its version (Gold or Silver, a team colour and so on). It was probably random, but nobody says.
- Party Hat Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle on Yellow, and Serebii’s Sandslash.
- Whether Chespin, Fennekin and Froakie from the Mega Finale GO Pass had the Mega background.
- Whether Into the Wild 2025 (Shadow Lugia, Ho-Oh, Tapus, Necrozma) had the same background as GO Wild Area 2025.
- Virizion and Pikipek on Tales of Transformation (only one source each).
- GO Wild Area 2026: the weekend (14–15 Nov) is still to come, and the 3-star Machamp may not count.
