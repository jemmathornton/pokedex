# Pokémon GO in-person events in Europe, 2016 – Nov 2026

Compiled 9 November 2026 from web sources (mainly Serebii, with Leek Duck, Pokémon GO Hub, Dittobase and official pokemongo.com posts as cross-checks). The list covers official, in-person Pokémon GO events and tie-ins held in Europe: ticketed events and free official ones. Global online-only events are left out.

- **No 2016 events.** The first official European in-person event Serebii records is the Chester Heritage Festival (July 2017).
- **Location backgrounds began in February 2023.** The first one was GO Tour: Hoenn – Las Vegas (18–19 Feb 2023), so nothing before that date has a background. The first European one is GO Fest 2023 London.
- **Background names** are as Serebii lists them. The exact in-game wording may differ slightly.
- **Background Pokémon:** only the listed Pokémon could get the background. Forms matter: for example, City Safari backgrounds go only on **Eevee (Explorer Hat)**, and Road Trip backgrounds go only on **Pikachu (Summer Style)**. The JSON uses base species names, so check this file for the form.
- **Debut shinies** means shinies that were released for the first time at that event. A dash means the sources name none.
- Machine-readable copy: `data/european-events.json` (79 entries: each National Trust site, ESA museum, Road Trip stop and 2017 Safari Zone city has its own entry).

## Notes

**Special (non-location) backgrounds tied to European in-person events.** Only one turned up: **LEGO Pokémon** (Pikachu). It was first given at GO Fest 2026 Copenhagen, which was run with LEGO, and then at the Aug–Sep 2026 LEGO store collaboration. The GO Fest 2024 Ultra Wormhole backgrounds and the GO Fest 2025 Zacian/Zamazenta backgrounds were global, not European in-person.

**GO Fest 2026 Copenhagen.** Serebii lists seven species for the background. Leek Duck describes it only for Mewtwo caught in the in-person Super Mega Raids at Fælledparken. Treat the wider list as unsure. Leek Duck calls shiny Wash Rotom a debut but does not say the same for Paldean Tauros.

**GO Fest 2025 Paris.** The park was Parc de Sceaux, just south of the city. Serebii also lists Charmander for this background. That is probably carried over from the September Mega Evolution Paris event (unsure).

**Mega Evolution Paris (Sep 2025).** This was the first GO Stamp Rally outside Asia, held around Westfield Forum des Halles and central Paris landmarks. It had two backgrounds ("Paris" and "Paris 2"). Charmander could be caught with them, and Charmeleon and Charizard keep the background when they evolve.

**City Safaris.** Four European City Safaris happened in 2023–2025: Barcelona 2023, then Milan, Amsterdam and Valencia in 2025. None was held in Europe in 2024. Three more ran in 2026: Lisbon, Marseille and Munich. Serebii's background list dates Amsterdam and Valencia to 13–14 Sep 2025, but the official post and event pages say **27–28 Sep 2025**. For Barcelona 2023, Serebii and Dittobase both show a background. Linking it to Explorer Hat Eevee comes from Serebii alone (unsure).

**Road Trip 2025.** These were free stops around a "GO Truck". Serebii's Road Trip page lists no Cologne background, but its background list and Dittobase do (unsure).

**Pokémon GO × European Space Agency (one background per museum):**
- Science Museum, London → "London Science Museum". Serebii also shows 2 Oct 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 for London (unsure).
- Cité de l'espace, Toulouse → "Cité de l'Espace"
- Deutsches Museum, Nuremberg → "Germany Space Center". The match to this museum is inferred.
- Museu de les Ciències, Valencia → "Ciutat de les Arts"
- Space Expo, Noordwijk → "Netherlands Space Expo"
- Euro Space Center, Transinne (Belgium) → "Euro Space Center"

Field research and boosted spawns ran 4–10 Oct 2026. Raids and Astronaut Pikachu run until 30 Apr 2027. ESA sites (ESTEC, ECSAT Harwell and others) had gameplay on 4–10 Oct but no background was listed for them.

**National Trust (18 Jul – 6 Sep 2026).** This was free. 140 sites got PokéStops, and 27 sites had raids, Timed Research and their own background. Each background is "National Trust *Site*" and goes on Treecko, Grovyle, Sceptile and the regional raid Pokémon:
- *Hoppip (south coast):* Cliveden, Mottisfont, Nymans, Polesden Lacey, Scotney Castle, Stowe
- *Sewaddle (Midlands & London):* Anglesey Abbey, Attingham Park, Belton, Calke Abbey, Clumber Park, Hanbury Hall, Hardwick, Wimpole
- *Pineco (North):* Dunham Massey, Fountains Abbey, Gibside, Lyme, Mount Stewart (N. Ireland)
- *Seedot (South West & Wales):* Chirk Castle, Killerton, Kingston Lacy, Lacock, Stourhead, Tredegar House, Trelissick, Tyntesfield. Serebii omits Sceptile for Tyntesfield, probably a typo.

**Safari Zone Europe 2017.** All seven were free events at Unibail-Rodamco shopping centres. The October dates were rescheduled from August after GO Fest Chicago's problems. Featured spawns included Kangaskhan, Chansey, Larvitar and Unown. There were no shiny debuts. The German stop was **Oberhausen**, not Dortmund; Dortmund's Safari Zone was in 2018.

**Safari Zone Liverpool.** It was first set for 17–19 Apr 2020 and postponed for COVID-19. It went ahead on 15–17 Oct 2021 as a hybrid event, with ticket-holder spawns anywhere and facilities in Sefton Park.

**Considered but not listed as separate events:**
- Europe International Championships 2025 (21–23 Feb) and 2026 (13–15 Feb), both at ExCeL London. Only Twitch-drop research was confirmed; no in-person GO content or background was found (unsure).
- European Regional Championships 2022–23, which had small sponsored-PokéStop research at various venues.
- "Old Friends, New Beginnings" in Turkey and Poland, which were country-wide language-launch events, not in person.
- No GO Tour, Wild Area or Worlds in-person event has been held in Europe. Wild Area venues were Fukuoka, Nagasaki, Sendai and Mexico City, and Worlds 2023–26 were outside Europe.
