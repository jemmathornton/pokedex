# Next time you play: suggestions

Written 12 Nov 2026 from your ticks in the app and current news (Leek Duck, pokemongo.com, Pokémon GO Hub). **Everything here is a suggestion.** Your ticks are still going in, so “you’re missing X” means “the app doesn’t have X ticked yet”: some of it you may already have. Search strings go in the search box above your Pokémon storage.

## 1. Tick what you already have (10 minutes, no walking)

Suggestion: before hunting, let the game tell you what to tick. So far the app has almost no Mega, Shadow, Purified, Dynamax, XXL/XXS, 4★, costume or background ticks, and only Bulbasaur, Venusaur and Charizard have them, so most of these are probably just not entered yet.

| Search | Shows | Tick in the app |
|---|---|---|
| `shadow` | your Shadows | Shadow |
| `purified` | your Purified | Purified |
| `xxl,xxs` | your XXL and XXS ones | XXL / XXS |
| `4*` | your 4★ (100%) ones | 4★ |
| `dynamax` / `gigantamax` | your Dynamax and G-Max ones | Dynamax / G-Max |
| `costume` | costumed Pokémon | Costumes page |
| `specialbackground` / `locationbackground` | Pokémon with backgrounds | Events page |

The game’s own Pokédex also has Mega, Shadow, Purified, XXL and XXS tabs, which are quicker for those boxes than the search. Quick add in the app takes lines like `charizard mega x` or `gengar shadow`.

## 4. Mega Evolve what you’ve caught

Suggestion: 59 Pokémon you’ve caught have a Mega in GO but no Mega tick (only Venusaur and Charizard have one). Doing any one Mega Evolution registers it.
- Search **`megaevolve&mega0`**: ones with enough Mega Energy now that have never Mega Evolved. Each is a free tick. (If `mega0` gives nothing on your phone, just `megaevolve`.)
- Mega Energy comes from Mega Raids (Banette, Metagross, Beedrill, Houndoom, Sableye, Manectric, Clefable: see the table) and from walking with a Mega as buddy. The first Mega Evolution costs the most (usually 200 Energy; a few 100 or 300); the button shows the exact cost.

## 5. Evolve to fill boxes

Suggestion: evolving keeps shiny and Lucky, and the game’s Pokédex never forgets a registration, so evolving even your only one is fine.
- **`evolve&evolvenew`**: ones you can evolve right now into something new for the Pokédex. 57 Pokémon aren’t ticked but their earlier stage is, e.g. Thwackey, Doublade, Cosmoem, Dottler, Morgrem, Drakloak, Dolliv, Naclstack, Tinkatuff (25 Candy each).
- **`shiny&evolve`**: 34 shiny gaps you could fill from a shiny you have, e.g. Krokorok, Doublade, Hattrem, Quaxwell, Dolliv, Tinkatuff (25 Candy), Unfezant, Beartic, Golurk, Dubwool, Ribombee (50).
- **`lucky&evolve`**: 76 Lucky gaps, e.g. Kakuna, Pidgeotto, Silcoon (12 Candy), Quilava, Croconaw, Togetic, Vigoroth, Luxio, Swadloon, Hakamo-o, Arctibax (25).
- Some need a task first, so make it your buddy or start it now: Pangoro (catch 32 Dark-type), Armarouge / Ceruledge (beat 30 Psychic / Ghost-type), Annihilape (beat 30 Psychic- or Ghost-type), Slurpuff (25 buddy treats), Pawmot (walk 25 km as buddy).

## 6. Purify spare Shadows

Suggestion: search **`shadow`**, keep one of each (for the Shadow box and raids) and purify a spare to fill Purified. It costs Stardust and Candy (from 1,000 and 1 up to 20,000 and 20 for legendaries; the button shows it). Shadows can’t be traded; Purified ones can, but as a Special Trade.

## 7. Lucky trades

Suggestion: 287 Pokémon you’ve caught have no Lucky tick.
- **Guaranteed Lucky**: a Pokémon that’s been in storage since 2020 or earlier is guaranteed to come out Lucky in a trade, up to 45 for each player (permanent since New Year 2026). Ask friends to send you their old ones of species you need Lucky, and send them yours. Your old tradeable ones: **`year2016-2020&!lucky&!traded&!shadow&!mythical`** (there is no official “tradeable” search: a traded Pokémon, a Shadow or a mythical can’t be traded).
- **Lucky Friends**: interact with Best Friends every day for a chance; a Lucky Friend’s next trade is guaranteed Lucky. Twilight Wishes (24–30 Nov) raises the chance.
- **Special Trades** (a shiny, a legendary or one new to your Pokédex) are 1 a day: 2 on Pawniard Community Day, 6 with the Wild Area ticket. Best Friends pay the least Stardust.

## 8. Other habits worth keeping

- **Male and female:** almost no ♂/♀ ticks yet. `+espurr&female` (the family, female only) is a quick check per species; 102 Pokémon look different as ♀.
- **XXL / XXS:** check new catches with `xxl,xxs` now and then; event days with lots of spawns are the easy time.
- **4★:** `4*` after a raid or Community Day.
