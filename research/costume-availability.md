## Unsure (one question for you)

- **Snorlax in a nightcap:** do you have a Pokémon GO Plus + linked to Pokémon Sleep? The only way found is the Special Research that comes with pairing a GO Plus + and tracking sleep in Pokémon Sleep (from July 2023). If you have never had a GO Plus +, it’s a no.

## Main doubts

- **The July 2026 Pikachu celebration does a lot of work.** The Special Anniversary Pikachu Celebration (13–20 Jul 2026, worldwide, free) brought back most of the old event Pikachu in the wild, one-star raids, research and the free GO Pass: the GO Tour hats (Brendan, May, Lucas, Dawn, Rei, Akari, Hilbert, Hilda, Nate, Rosa, Red, Leaf, Ethan, Lyra, Calem, Serena), Meloetta hat, Gracidea, Shaymin scarf, the five crowns, Worlds 2022–2025, Safari hat, Dapper and Formal. Some of these were possible research rewards (a random pick from a few), so getting each one needed luck as well as playing that week. That is why they are “local”, not “event”.
- **Older seasonal costumes** (2016–2025 Halloween, holiday, spring, New Year, Fashion Week and anniversary ones) are marked yes from the costume’s release history (Leek Duck’s Shiny list dates and well-known worldwide events), not from a page for each one.
- **Baseball-jersey Pikachu:** all sources tie it to the Japanese baseball tie-in; none says outright “Japan only”.
- **Cowboy-hat Snorlax:** found only at the in-person GO Fest 2022 events; I found no later return, but didn’t find a page ruling one out.
- **Machamp in a modern jacket:** GO Wild Area 2026: Global is this weekend (14–15 Nov) and the Into the Wild GO Pass runs to 15 Nov, so it’s “yes” only once you play them.
- **“May 2023” Eevee:** an odd-case game code with no shiny and no Leek Duck name; it is probably an unused copy of the City Safari explorer-hat Eevee. Either way not one you could get (no City Safari).
- **“Doctor” Pikachu** is Pikachu, Ph.D. (the game calls the form PIKACHU_DOCTOR): it comes from the Platinum PokéStop Showcase medal (100 wins), so it needs that many Showcase wins.
- **Gimmighoul “(9)” and “(10)”:** taken to be the 9th and 10th anniversary Coin Bag/Golden Lure Gimmighoul (the dates match); Leek Duck doesn’t spell it out.
