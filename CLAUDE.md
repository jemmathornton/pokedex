A private Pokédex for Jemma’s Pokémon GO collection: every Pokémon and form in GO, with boxes for caught (male and female), shiny (male and female), lucky and 4★; costumes she chooses to collect; and the in-person European events she has been to, with their location backgrounds. Started 9 Nov 2026 in the style of her other apps (`Documents\JemmasJournals\sim-world`, her Sims site, and `Documents\JemmasJournals\library`, her library): a static site plus a small local server that saves her ticks. Built so other Pokémon games can be added later. Open things are in `TODO.md`; everything waiting on her is in `for-jemma/your-list.md`.
