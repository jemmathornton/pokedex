# Jemma’s Pokédex

A private Pokédex for Jemma’s Pokémon GO collection: every Pokémon and form in GO, with boxes for caught (male and female), shiny (male and female), lucky and 4★; costumes she chooses to collect; and the in-person European events she has been to, with their location backgrounds. Started 9 Nov 2026 in the style of her other apps (`Documents\SimTracker`, her Sims site, and `Documents\HomeLibrary`, her library): a static site plus a small local server that saves her ticks. Built so other Pokémon games can be added later. Open things are in `TODO.md`; everything waiting on her is in `for-jemma/your-list.md`.

## Working rules (as for her other apps)

- **Never edit Jemma’s own files without asking** (Downloads, Documents, Pictures…). Copy them into the project and work on the copy.
- **British English** in everything shown; **curly apostrophes** in displayed text (ids, links and data keys stay straight). Pokémon, form, event and costume names keep the game’s or the source’s spelling.
- **Short, plain answers.** She reviews by looking at the app, not by reading reports.
- **Obvious bugs** can be fixed straight away; **anything needing her judgement** (what to track, which costumes or events, layout, colours) goes on `for-jemma/your-list.md` as **Do** or **Decide**.
- **Show, don’t ask, for looks:** layout or design questions get a screenshot of each option at 1600px (her window), never text-only options.
- **No AI-generated art.** Pokémon pictures are the game’s own (PokeMiners’ copy of the game files); icons are Lucide (ISC, `site/js/LICENSE-lucide.txt`); the app icon is drawn from plain shapes by `tools/make_icons.py`.
- **Icons only from the sets her apps use** (Lucide, plus Lucide Lab, Tabler or Phosphor where Lucide has nothing), and **never a real logo, brand mark or awareness ribbon** (Tabler’s brand-* icons are out; 9 Nov she spotted the X/Twitter logo among the options). - **No made-up text.** Names and descriptions come from the game or the sources below, word for word. A costume Leek Duck doesn’t name gets its game code tidied, nothing more.
- **Suggestions aren’t facts:** label anything Claude proposes as a suggestion.
- **Her ticks are hers:** never change `data/collection/go.json` by hand or by script without her asking. Tests and checks use port 8031, which writes to the same file, so remove any test ticks afterwards (or check on a copy).
- **Box colours (her picks, 9 Nov):** white text on every box, never mixed with black; bright, not dull; no gender stereotypes (no blue/pink for ♂/♀); Dynamax and G-Max follow the game’s dark red-pink; balanced to one palette (similar brightness). ## The look: Cards in a binder (her pick, 21 Nov)

Her pick P3b (“Cards in a binder”, with the softer shiny window), chosen from mock-ups in Documents\JemmasJournals (research/app-styles/pokedex, pictures in for-jemma/concepts/app-styles) and built into site.css 21 Nov, section “The binder look” at the end. Pokémon, costume and background cards are collector’s cards: a frame in the page’s region colour (`--rc`, app.js `REGION_COLOURS`), the name across the top, the picture in an art window tinted by its first type (her pick, 21 Nov), the number bottom right (a star for legendary/mythical). Shiny ticked = foil border on the art window and a faint pastel rainbow, no stripes; never without the Shiny box ticked (her answer, 21 Nov; checked in the tests). The foil shows whatever tab is on, on background cards too (`.card.shiny`); a form-by-form Pokémon with some forms got isn’t faded (`.card.some`) (21 Nov review). Not caught (with a tab on: not that box; costumes and backgrounds: their Caught box) = grey frame and faded grey picture. Home’s regions are set cards; a Pokémon’s page has its picture as a card; the sidebar is a padded red cover (shading only: **no line may cross the sidebar’s items**, her note), with a soft binder shadow where it meets the page; the banner totals hang from its bottom edge as index tabs. A banner with no tabs (a Pokémon’s page) has 32px under its chips, as much as above (her note, 21 Nov: the chips sat on the edge; site.css `.hero.withshot:not(:has(> .stats))`). Dark mode is the same look on dark card stock (`--card-stock` and friends in the section’s `:root[data-mode="dark"]` blocks). Narrow windows and phones (900px and under, her ask 21 Nov: like her other apps): a top bar with a Menu button, the sidebar a drawer over the page (closes on a link, Escape, tapping outside, Enter in search; app.js `setMenu`); on phones the totals sit in two rows of seven and a Pokémon’s page is one column.
