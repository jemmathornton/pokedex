## New ways to put your collection in
- **Fast tick** (new, a trial; sidebar under More): a whole region at once, laid out like the game’s own Pokédex.
  1. Open the same tab and region in the game on your phone (Pokémon, Shiny, Lucky, 100%, Shadow, Purified, G-Max, Mega).
  2. Click every Pokémon that’s in colour there.
  3. Check the count at the top (e.g. “140 / 151”) matches the game’s.

  There’s a **Tick all left** button for when you have nearly all of a region; then untick the few you’re missing. Picture: `for-jemma/fast-tick.png`.
- **Reading a Pokédex recording** (a trial). Record one Pokédex tab and one region with Android’s screen recorder, scrolling slowly. The tool then works out which Pokémon are in colour and suggests the ticks; nothing is ticked until you say so. It read 45 out of 45 Pokémon right on real screenshots from the new Pokédex. It hasn’t seen a real recording yet, because YouTube blocked the test videos.
- Quick add and the Calcy importer are still there as before.

**Easiest first step:** open **Fast tick → Kanto → Lucky** with your phone’s Pokédex on the Lucky tab, and see how it feels. If you’d rather not click, send one short recording of that tab instead.
