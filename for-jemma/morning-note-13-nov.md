# Good morning (13 Nov 2026)

Overnight: research, and the changes that didn’t need your say. Your ticks weren’t touched. All tests pass.

## Done
- **Sharper Kanto and Johto maps:** the same painting, now from a copy twice the size. They look the same, just crisper on your screen. Kalos, Hoenn and Unova: no bigger copy of those exact pictures exists anywhere I could find. Larger Kalos maps all have clouds, labels or the game logo.
- **Only the impossible is left out**, now taken from the game’s own data:
  - **Lucky:** left out only for the 22 Pokémon the game won’t let you trade: 20 mythicals, plus **Zygarde and Eternatus**. Meltan, Melmetal and Pecharunt can be traded, so they count.
  - **Shadow and Purified:** count for every Pokémon. My earlier guess that mythicals can’t be Shadow was wrong: Shadow Darkrai was in raids last year.
  - **XXL/XXS:** Pumpkaboo and Gourgeist come in one fixed size per form, so they can never be XXS, and only their Super Size form can be XXL. Every other Pokémon can be both.
  - **Dynamax:** only Zacian, Zamazenta and Eternatus can never Dynamax (as before).
- **Fast tick:** if you tick Shiny (or any box) on a Pokémon that wasn’t Caught, and later untick it there, its Caught comes off too. This only works from now on, because there’s no record of earlier ones.
- **Cards** follow the game’s Pokédex order like everywhere else.
