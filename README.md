# Jemma’s Pokédex

**To open it:** double-click **Jemma’s Pokédex** on your Desktop (also in the Start menu, and in this folder). It opens in its own window.

**Ticking:** every Pokémon has boxes for caught (♂ and ♀), shiny (♂ and ♀), lucky (the clover) and 4★. Click a box to tick it; it saves straight away. Click a Pokémon’s picture to see its other versions (female, shiny). Dashed boxes can’t be ticked yet (no shiny in GO, or a mythical that can never be lucky).

**Costumes:** on the Costumes page, pick the ones you want with “Collect this”; they then get their own boxes.

**Events:** on the Events page, tick “I went” on the European events you’ve been to; events with a location background then get boxes for the Pokémon that could have it.

**What happens by itself:** every Sunday at 9:40 (or when the PC is next on) everything is saved to a backup zip in Documents\PokedexBackups.

**To change anything:** open Claude Code in this folder and ask. New Pokémon or shinies in GO: say “update the Pokédex”.

What’s in this folder:

- `site` – the app itself
- `data` – what the app shows, and your ticks (`data/collection/go.json`)
- `tools` – the scripts Claude runs
- `research` – notes: the European events list, which Pokémon are out
- `for-jemma` – your list: things waiting for you to do or decide
